The invitation photo chapter is enabled in `photo-config.js` and displays five optimized JPEGs from `assets/photos/`.

| `our-story-06.jpg` | `photos/15.jpg` | Enhanced playful bouquet portrait | `50% 50%` |

`share-ye-nang.jpg` is a 1200 × 630 social-sharing crop derived from enhanced source `photos/3.jpg`. It is referenced by the Open Graph and Twitter image metadata in `index.html`.

The website copies are resized and metadata-free for faster loading. Preserve the original full-resolution photographs outside the repository. When replacing a photograph, keep its invitation filename or update `photo-config.js`, provide English, Thai and Burmese alternative text, and check both the 5:6 feature crop and 4:5 phone crop.
